Stream Duo Support

Getting started

Grant camera and microphone access, open Studio settings, choose your service and paste the server address and stream key from that service’s creator dashboard. Save the destination, then choose Go live. A preview is not a live broadcast. The default local test destination is for development; replace it before publishing to an audience.

Connection problems

Prefer the RTMPS address provided by your service. Verify your key and that the service accepts external encoders. Output is 720p at 30 fps. Two destinations need additional upload capacity and power. Stream health distinguishes connection state and reconnects; it cannot measure viewer latency or viewer count.

Interruptions and recording

Calls, audio interruptions and backgrounding stop an in-app broadcast. Return and choose Go live again. System game broadcasts run in the ReplayKit extension and must be stopped using the system broadcast control. Face camera in another app depends on device and OS support. Delete local recordings in the library; exported copies remain in Files or Photos until removed there.

Chat safety

Use the options next to a chat message to hide it, block its author locally or open the reporting flow. Manage blocks and filter words in Studio settings. Twitch reports must be completed on Twitch. Local controls do not ban accounts on Twitch or remove material already delivered to viewers.

Purchase recovery

The overlay pack is a one-time Apple in-app purchase. Use Restore purchases with the same Apple Account. A pending purchase unlocks only after Apple approves it. A refund revokes the entitlement. Pricing is provided by Apple and varies by storefront.

Contact support

Contact https://github.com/Gamedesigner/streamduo-support/issues/new/choose. Include your app version, device, iOS version and a description of the problem. Never include stream keys, passwords, payment information or private video.
