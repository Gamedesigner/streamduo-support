Stream Duo Privacy Policy
Effective October 21, 2026

Your studio stays on your device

Stream Duo has no developer-operated account system, analytics, advertising or tracking service. We do not receive your stream keys, camera feed, microphone audio, recordings, chat history or imported assets on a developer-operated server.

Live video and audio

When you choose Go live, the app sends your composed video and audio directly to the streaming destination(s) you select. System game broadcasting uses ReplayKit and can capture the contents and audio of other apps. Review your screen and notification settings before broadcasting. Your chosen service controls its own processing, retention and public distribution. RTMPS encrypts transport; plain RTMP does not. Stream Duo does not make an RTMP destination private.

On-device storage and deletion

Stream keys are stored in this device’s Keychain, shared only with the app’s broadcast extension. Delete a saved key in Studio settings. Preferences, overlay designs, blocked chat authors and filter words stay in local app storage. Recordings remain in the in-app library until you delete them. Imported music and logos are copied into app storage; removing a copy does not remove your original. Copies exported to Files or Photos must be deleted there separately. Some app files may be included in device backups according to your Apple settings. Removing the app deletes its local files, but Keychain items can survive app deletion; delete keys in settings first.

Twitch chat and safety

Chat is optional. When enabled, your device connects directly to Twitch and requests the public channel you enter. Twitch receives your network address and connection information. The app retains only a bounded recent chat history in memory. Local blocks, hidden messages and word filters affect this device and the chat overlay; they do not ban accounts on Twitch or recall frames already broadcast or recorded. Reporting opens Twitch’s tools, where you review and submit the report yourself. Opening the report screen does not send a report. See Twitch’s privacy notice and community guidelines for that service’s practices.

Purchases

Apple processes the free 14-day Trial and one-time Studio purchases. Stream Duo reads Apple-verified entitlements and original trial purchase dates to determine Studio access. Reinstalling does not reset a trial. An earlier overlay purchase retains its original overlay entitlement. We do not receive your card details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to purchases and store services.

Permissions and your choices

Camera and microphone permission support capture. Photos access is add-only and requested when you export a recording there. Files access uses the system picker. Local-network access supports destinations on your own network. You can revoke permissions in Apple Settings, disconnect chat, stop publishing, remove imported assets and delete recordings or keys in the app.

Contact

For privacy and support requests, contact https://github.com/Gamedesigner/streamduo-support/issues/new/choose. Never send stream keys or private recordings. If using GitHub Issues, your request is public. Never post keys, personal/payment information or private recordings.
